<p>OpenExplorer is a fast, minimalist browser built on the system WebView (Chromium), with no Google services and no telemetry.</p>

<p><b>Privacy</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Ad and tracker blocking (built-in list plus the full StevenBlack list on demand), cookie banner hiding.</li>
<li>Incognito tabs in a separate WebView profile.</li>
<li>Tab containers (e.g. “Work”, “Personal”) – each with its own cookies and site data, e.g. two accounts on the same site at once.</li>
<li>HTTPS-only mode, DNT/GPC, pop-up and third-party cookie blocking, clearing data on exit.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Passwords</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Built-in password manager: offers to save after sign-in, fills with one tap, generates strong passwords.</li>
<li>AES-256 encryption with a key from Android Keystore, unlocked with fingerprint or PIN.</li>
<li>CSV import and export (Edge, Chrome, Firefox, Bitwarden).</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Browsing</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Start page with site logos in a grid of any size, wallpapers, ready-made layouts.</li>
<li>Address bar at the top or bottom, hiding on scroll, with suggestions and search shortcuts (!yt, !w, !gh…).</li>
<li>Reader mode, read aloud, translation, offline pages, screenshots, QR codes, print to PDF.</li>
<li>Page source viewer with syntax highlighting and a list of resources.</li>
<li>Per-site settings: blocking exceptions, JavaScript, desktop mode, text size, custom CSS and script.</li>
</ul>

<p>Interface in English and Polish. Source code under the MIT license.</p>
